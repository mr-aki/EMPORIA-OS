# Community Code of Conduct

Emporia OS is a collaborative project. We welcome people with different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints.

Expected behavior includes being respectful, giving constructive feedback, acknowledging others' work, and focusing disagreement on ideas and evidence. Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, doxxing, and personal attacks are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions that violate this standard and may restrict participation when necessary. Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Please avoid sharing private or sensitive information in reports.
